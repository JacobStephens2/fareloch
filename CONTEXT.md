# Fareloch domain glossary

Terms used across the server, guest mode and the views. The Nutrition module (`server/src/nutrition.ts`) owns the nutrition calculations named here.

- **Macros**: the calories, carbs, protein and fat of one quantity of food, as a single value (`Macros` in the Nutrition module).
- **Mounted lifetime**: the permanent ownership of a connected mounted host and its fixed owned nodes, ending on explicit disposal or departure of those nodes or their captured mounted ancestors. Retirement releases listeners and registered cleanup while accepted persistence retains its existing owner.
- **Macros entry**: the calorie, carbs, protein and fat controls, parsed values, automatic calorie calculation, user calorie override and input notifications owned by one mounted Quick Add, Custom Food or Manual macros form. Its lifetime ends on replacement or parent disposal; submitted persistence belongs to its parent session.
- **Per-serving nutrition**: a recipe's Macros divided by its total servings; from Manual macros when set, otherwise from its ingredients. A total servings of 0 or blank counts as 1.
- **Manual macros**: whole-recipe Macros entered by hand, overriding the ingredient sum. Blank manual grams count as 0.
- **Unit scale**: how many default servings one household measure is (measure gram weight / food serving size).
- **Stored rounding / Display rounding**: 0.1 for every field (what `meal_logs` holds) / whole kcal and 0.1 g (what recipe `perServing` and the modals show).
- **Food search session**: the mounted food query controls, search feedback, saved and external result presentation, and selection through external saving within Log Food or a Recipe editor. Query changes and activation control which reads and rows are current; inactive queries resume on activation. A submitted external save belongs to the live parent mount and can complete across query, tab, mode or modal changes. Departure or disposal permanently ends its selection callbacks and alerts, while persistence finishes.
- **Saved Food list session**: the read, loading/empty/error feedback, saved Food rows, My Foods Create action eligibility and selection eligibility owned by one mounted explicit Recent or My Foods list. Its lifetime ends permanently on replacement or parent disposal; Food search and submitted writes retain their own lifetimes.
- **Log Food session**: The selected date and Meal type, tabs, food and recipe selection, Meal add sessions, other forms, scanner, DOM and pending work owned by one mounted Log Food screen. It opens and retires Meal add sessions and owns their successful Dashboard return eligibility, including after an add form closes or is replaced. Explicit disposal or departure of its root, fixed owned nodes or their captured mounted ancestors permanently retires it; only its live mount may act, publish or navigate. Accepted persistence retains its captured inputs and owner and may finish after retirement.
- **Meal add session**: The captured Food or Recipe, controls, quantity and Macros preview, Add action and feedback owned by one open Log Food meal-add form. Retirement permanently ends controls and local feedback, while accepted persistence and successful Dashboard return retain their Log Food session owner.
- **Barcode scanner session**: The scan controls, camera attempts, optional constraints, pending release and accepted-barcode delivery owned by one mounted Log Food screen. Disposal or departure of its captured root, fixed controls or mounted ancestors permanently retires it. Camera release finishes after retirement; barcode lookup belongs to the Log Food session.
- **Recipe editor session**: The draft, ingredient selection, modes, DOM and pending work owned by one mounted Recipe editor. Explicit disposal or departure of its root, fixed owned nodes or their captured mounted ancestors permanently retires it; only its live mount may act, publish or navigate. Accepted persistence retains its captured identity, values and owner and may finish after retirement.
- **Recipe list session**: The read, loading/empty/error feedback, Recipe rows, Per-serving nutrition presentation and selection eligibility owned by one mounted Recipes list or Log Food Recipes tab. Its lifetime ends permanently on replacement or parent disposal.
- **Food quantity selection**: The quantity, selected default serving or household measure, Unit scale, labels and Macros previews owned by one open food or ingredient quantity form; its lifetime ends permanently on host or captured ancestor departure, replacement or parent disposal. It belongs to a Meal add session or Recipe editor session and uses the Nutrition module's Unit scale for household measures.
- **Daily checks**: The independent, per-date Creatine 5g and Carotenoids + Fat 3x completion flags; they do not affect Macros or workout targets.
- **Meal edit session**: The captured meal, controls, quantity and Macros previews, actions and feedback owned by one open Dashboard meal editor. Retirement is permanent. Accepted persistence retains its captured inputs and owner; successful-write refresh belongs to the Dashboard date session.
- **Dashboard date session**: The selected date, meal data, workout state, Daily checks state, interactions and feedback, and pending work owned by one mounted Dashboard. It opens and retires Meal edit sessions and owns successful-write refresh eligibility for the original live date selection, including after an editor closes or is replaced. Explicit disposal or departure of its root, fixed owned nodes or their captured mounted ancestors permanently retires it; only current work for that mount and selection may publish. Accepted persistence retains its captured inputs and owner and finishes after retirement.
- **Weight session**: The forms, weight history, trend and waist comparison, chart, DOM and pending work owned by one mounted Weight screen. Explicit disposal or departure of its root, fixed owned nodes or their captured mounted ancestors permanently retires it; only the newest refresh in the live mount may publish data, and only that mount may publish action feedback. Accepted persistence retains its captured inputs and owner and finishes after retirement.
- **Settings form session**: The Profile, Daily Targets, Workout-Day Targets, Phase Presets, and Change Password forms, their DOM, listeners, and feedback timers owned by one mounted Settings screen. Explicit disposal or departure of its root, fixed owned nodes or their captured mounted ancestors permanently retires it. Only that live mount may publish form feedback or mutate its controls; accepted persistence retains its captured inputs and owner, and completed profile and target saves still publish application user state after retirement.
- **Password recovery session**: The Forgot Password or Set New Password form, captured reset token when applicable, DOM, listeners, pending feedback, and delayed login navigation owned by one mount. Explicit disposal or departure of its root, fixed owned nodes or their captured mounted ancestors permanently retires it; only that live mount may publish feedback or view-owned navigation. Accepted persistence retains its captured input, reset token and owner and finishes after retirement.
- **Login and Registration session**: The controls, captured submissions, pending state, feedback, guest entry and view-owned navigation of one mounted Login or Registration screen. Explicit disposal or departure of its root, fixed owned nodes or their captured mounted ancestors permanently retires it, ending actions and view effects. Accepted authentication retains its captured inputs and owner, and application user publication finishes after retirement.
